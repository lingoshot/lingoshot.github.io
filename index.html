Never learned English? Start here.

A day-by-day course for beginners. Open the app, tap today's lesson, and in about ten minutes you've learned something you can actually say.

  • 4 short lessons a day. Each day has one topic — greetings, family, food, shopping, your home, your job — and a clear goal.
  • A path you can see. A level map shows your days, stars and what comes next. New days keep arriving.
  • Reviews that stick. Finish a day with a quick round on the words you just learned, and get a reminder when the next day opens.
  • Your language, your progress. Lessons explain things in your own language, and your progress is saved to your account.

Ten ways to learn

Every mode trains something different — from reaction speed to real conversation. It never gets boring.

🎯

Shooting Tir

A word flies across the screen — recall the translation and type it before it's gone. Smart spaced repetition brings it back at the right moment and locks it into memory.

🔗

Match Pairs

Tap a word, then its translation. Clear the board by matching every pair — quick, simple, and great for reviewing what you've learned.

🔍

Fillwords

Your words are hidden in a grid of letters. Track them all down like a seasoned tracker — a workout for attention and spelling.

💬

Phrases

Build real, everyday sentences word by word — 750+ phrases for shopping, banking, travel, and small talk. Speak like a local, not a textbook.

💡

Idiom Quiz

Feel like you're on "Who Wants to Be a Millionaire": pick the right answer and crack the meaning of tricky English expressions.

🧱

Chunks

Ready-made blocks like "Could you…", "Let me know" or "have to" — each with a translation, examples and audio. Learn how English is really put together.

🤖

AI Chat Beta

Practice real conversations — a hotel, a taxi, a shop, a work meeting or any topic you choose. The AI answers like a real person and shows how to say it better.

🧩

Morph Bubble

Add a suffix or ending to a word — and one word turns into several. Your vocabulary grows the clever way.

📖

Funny Stories

Short texts you can read and listen to. Hit an unfamiliar word — tap it, see the translation, and send it straight to "Shooting Tir" to learn it.

📚

Books

Longer stories told chapter by chapter, so you always have a reason to come back. Pin your current book to the top, and finished chapters light up.
